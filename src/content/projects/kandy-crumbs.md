- An unofficial concept demo built to pitch a new website to Kandy Crumbs, a home bakery in Jodhpur (custom cakes, brownies, cookies and desserts).
- The full menu is searchable by category; tapping items adds them to a printed-style receipt that is sent to the bakery on WhatsApp — no checkout or payment gateway needed.
- A five-step custom cake studio (occasion → cake → design → about you → review) turns a customer's idea into a ready-to-send request.
- Prices come from the bakery's own printed menu (excluding GST); a festive gift box is clearly marked as a concept item.
- Kept out of search engines until the client approves it for launch.
