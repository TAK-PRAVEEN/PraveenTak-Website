- One bottle and almost nothing else in the hero; the page then turns the bottle into the story — milk, forest, farm, breed.
- A seven-step "cow to bottle" journey, natural-history breed plates, a living traceability map and a sixteen-check quality card.
- An SVG logo that writes and un-writes itself, line-mask headline reveals, and motion that respects reduced-motion preferences.
- The mobile layout is designed separately, not shrunk from desktop.
- Explored 55 documented design styles with JSON tokens before committing to the final direction.
